Buying from outside Japan

Most of these figures sell on Japanese sites that only deliver inside Japan. From abroad you buy through a proxy service: it buys the item for you, receives it at its warehouse in Japan and ships it to you, several purchases in one box if you like.

What you'll pay
  • The item price, plus the seller's shipping to the proxy's warehouse if the listing doesn't include it
  • The proxy's fee: about ¥300–¥500 per item or per order on most services (see the services below)
  • International shipping, by weight and box size: combining several figures in one parcel saves a lot
  • Your country's import duty or tax when it arrives

Which service for which site
Yahoo! AuctionsBuyee is Yahoo's own partner: search and bid in English. ZenMarket and FROM JAPAN also bid for you. Every figure has "Search on Buyee" links.

MercariIn the US, Taiwan and Hong Kong, Mercari's own Global app buys from Mercari Japan directly, no proxy needed. Elsewhere: Buyee, ZenMarket or Neokyo.
Square Enix e-STORENeeds a proxy that buys from any shop by link: Remambo and Japan Rabbit have guides for this store; ZenMarket and Buyee accept shop links too. The store refuses foreign addresses and overseas connections. For pre-orders the proxy orders when you ask and holds it until release; ask first about lottery or member-only sales.
Hobby SearchShips worldwide from its English shop; no proxy needed (it won't ship to forwarding addresses).
SootangA Japanese shop: use a proxy that buys from any shop by link.
